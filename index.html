FAQs

Questions dealers ask us

What is PDM fertilizer?

PDM is Potash Derived from Molasses. It is a potash fertilizer recovered from sugar industry molasses, with 14.5% water-soluble K₂O.

Is PDM better than MOP?

PDM has less potash per bag (14.5% against about 60% in MOP) but no chloride, and it adds organic matter. It suits chloride-sensitive crops. Compare the two on cost per kg of K₂O, not cost per bag.

How many bags of PDM per acre?

The label dose is 300 to 400 kg per hectare, which is about two and a half to three 50 kg bags per acre.

Can I mix PDM with other fertilizers?

Yes. PDM can be applied together with CNF PROM and CNF Booster as a basal dose.

What is the potash fertilizer price in India?

Rates change with MOP import costs and your location. Ask us for today's rate per 50 kg bag and we reply within 24 hours.

Is CNF a potash fertilizer manufacturer?

Yes. CNF makes PDM 14.5% at its own plant on Shahabad Road, Karnataka, and tests every batch before dispatch.

Do you supply potash fertilizer wholesale?

Yes. Dealers, distributors and farmer producer organisations can order CNF PDM in bulk. Share the quantity and delivery point and we will quote a rate per 50 kg bag.

Do I need a licence to sell fertilizer?

Yes. You need authorisation from your state agriculture department under the Fertiliser (Control) Order, 1985. CNF gives you the principal certificate once your dealership is approved.
